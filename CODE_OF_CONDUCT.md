# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge
We pledge to make participation in this project a welcoming and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards
Positive behavior includes being respectful, welcoming differing viewpoints, accepting constructive criticism, focusing on what is best for the community and showing empathy.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insults, discriminatory language, personal attacks, publishing private information without consent, and unprofessional sexual attention.

## Enforcement
Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers. Reports will be reviewed and handled respectfully and confidentially.
